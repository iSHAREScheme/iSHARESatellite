# Licensing and Legal Assurance Statement iSHARE Reference Software

## 1. Status and Authority

This Licensing and Legal Assurance Statement (the “Statement”) is formally issued and approved by the Board of Stichting iSHARE Foundation (the “Foundation”). The Foundation is the legal steward, maintainer and copyright holder of the iSHARE framework and reference software. This Statement expresses the binding licensing intent of the Foundation with respect to the publication, use, modification and commercial exploitation of such software.

This Statement is intended to provide legal certainty to enterprises, public bodies, service providers and software vendors relying on the iSHARE reference software in production environments.

## 2. Designation of iSHARE Reference Software

The Foundation designates the following components as official iSHARE reference software:

A. Licensed under GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0):

- Participant Register / Participant Credential Issuer

B. Unlicensed

- Authorisation Register / Data Rights Credential Issuer
- iSHARE Connector (Service Provider & Service Consumer components)

C. Licensed under Apache License, Version 2.0:

- iSHARE Onboarding Portal

## 3. Purpose and Deliberate Scope of AGPL-3.0

The Foundation deliberately applies AGPL-3.0 exclusively to trust-critical core components of the iSHARE Trust Framework. The purpose of this choice is to ensure that modifications affecting security, cryptographic correctness, trust enforcement and protocol interoperability remain open, auditable and available to the ecosystem as a whole.

AGPL-3.0 applies solely to the iSHARE core software itself and to derivative works thereof, as defined under applicable copyright law. The license is not intended, and shall not be construed, to extend beyond this scope.

## 4. Explicit Legal Assurance for Independent and Commercial Software

The Foundation hereby expressly confirms and provides legal assurance that software systems which interact with iSHARE reference software exclusively via documented and published APIs, without linking against, embedding, or incorporating AGPL-licensed code, do not constitute derivative works of the iSHARE core software.

Accordingly, such software systems:

- May be developed, licensed and distributed under any license, including proprietary licenses;
- Do not trigger any disclosure or copyleft obligations under AGPL-3.0;
- May be operated internally or offered commercially, including as managed services;
- May lawfully compete in the market on functionality, performance, usability and commercial terms.

## 5. Apache-2.0 Licensed Components

Components published under the Apache License, Version 2.0, including the iSHARE Onboarding Portal, are independent software products. They do not include AGPL-licensed code and interact with AGPL-licensed components solely through API-based communication.

The Foundation confirms that Apache-2.0 licensed components do not become subject to AGPL-3.0 and may be freely modified, extended, embedded and commercialised without reciprocal licensing obligations.

## 6. Mandatory AGPL-3.0 Coverage

For the avoidance of doubt, the following activities constitute the creation of derivative works of AGPL-licensed iSHARE software and therefore fall mandatorily within the scope of AGPL-3.0:

- Any modification of AGPL-licensed source code;
- Addition of functionality within the same codebase or runtime environment;
- Static or dynamic linking against AGPL-licensed libraries;
- Introduction of generic, reusable or ecosystem-relevant trust functionality into core components.

Where such derivative works are made available for use over a network, the corresponding source code must be made available under AGPL-3.0 in accordance with the license terms.

## 7. Security, Trust and Interoperability

Modifications affecting security controls, cryptographic mechanisms, credential handling, authorisation logic or protocol compliance are deemed trust-critical by the Foundation and shall always remain subject to AGPL-3.0. This requirement is fundamental to safeguarding the integrity of the iSHARE ecosystem.

## 8. Legal Effect and Reliance

This Statement does not amend, replace or override the applicable open-source license texts. However, it constitutes the official licensing interpretation, intent and assurance of the Foundation and may be reasonably relied upon by adopters, implementers and commercial parties when assessing licensing risk and designing system architectures.

This Statement has been formally approved and adopted by the Board of Stichting iSHARE Foundation and is published alongside the iSHARE reference software.
